Know the Signs of Heatstroke

Severe Headache
Dizziness
Nausea
No Sweating
Confusion
High Body Temp

If you or someone shows these signs — move to a cool place immediately and seek emergency medical help.

💊 ORS — Oral Rehydration Solution

Prepare at home to restore electrolytes quickly:

🚰 1 Litre Clean Water
+
🍬 6 tsp Sugar
+
🧂 ½ tsp Salt

Mix well and sip slowly throughout the day.
